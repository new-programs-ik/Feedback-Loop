# 📖 Feedback Loop — User Guide

**How to turn a low-rated class recording into ready-to-send instructor feedback in about 5 minutes.**

Written for anyone on the team — no technical background needed. Works for every program:
**ML, Agentic, FDE, LevelUp, B2B, PwC**, and any new course you add yourself.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open https://feedback-loop-ten.vercel.app
2. Click **Continue with Google** and pick your **@interviewkickstart.com** account.
3. That's it — every IK staff account gets access automatically. No sign-up, no password.

## 2. The dashboard — your home screen

It answers three questions at a glance: how much has been analysed, what is waiting for review, and
what it all costs.

- **The four counters** — classes analysed · drafts waiting for review · approved feedback · classes
  flagged for re-teaching.
- **AI spending** — exact cost, this month and all-time. Nothing hidden; a typical class is $0.50–0.70.
- **AI engine status** — the small dot under the welcome line. Green = ready. Just opening the
  dashboard **wakes the engine**, so your first analysis of the day starts faster.
- **New analysis** — the button you'll use most, top right.

## 3. First decide: does this class need analysis — and which type?

Don't analyse everything. The team rule:

> **Class rating → Rating participation % → Analysis type**

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Rating **above 4.5** | **No analysis needed** in most cases. Only if a PM asks or there's a specific reason — then Transcript is enough. |
| Rating **below 4.5** and **≥ 80%** of attendees rated it | **Video Analysis** — the signal is strong, get the full picture. |
| Rating **below 4.5** and **< 80%** rated it | **Transcript Analysis** — enough for a first read. |
| Any **escalation** or specific issue reported | **Video Analysis**, always. |

**Rating participation %** = (learners who rated ÷ learners who attended) × 100.
Example: 10 attended, 8 rated → 80%.

> 💡 **You don't have to remember this.** The New Analysis page has a **built-in helper**: type the
> rating, attended and rated numbers, and it tells you which analysis to run — and switches video on
> for you with one click.

## 4. Copy the class's Vimeo link from UpLevel

The analysis needs the recording's **Vimeo link**, which lives in IK's **UpLevel** platform:

1. Open **UpLevel** → left sidebar → **Resources → Videos**.
2. Find your class (search by class name and date) and open it.
3. You land on the **Basic Details** tab. Scroll down past "YouTube URL".
4. Copy the link in the **VIMEO URL** box — it looks like `https://vimeo.com/123456789`.

> 🔜 **Coming soon:** this step will be automated — the tool will look the link up itself. For now,
> copy-paste it.

## 5. Run a new analysis

Click **New analysis** and fill the form top to bottom. Only four things are required: course, topic,
date, and the recording.

1. **Course** — pick yours. Not listed? Choose **➕ Other** and type the name; it's created instantly.
2. **Class topic, instructor, date** — instructor names autocomplete.
3. **Class type** — Live class or Assignment review (ARS). Each has its own checklist.
4. **Avg rating & # ratings** — from your dashboard. The rating appears in the instructor note.
5. **Agenda** *(optional)* — paste the planned items; the AI checks coverage against them.
6. **The recording** — paste the **Vimeo link** from step 4, or upload a `.vtt` / `.srt` transcript.
7. **🎬 Analyze the video too** — tick when the helper recommends Video. Adds ~$0.20 and a few
   minutes, and catches camera/screen problems that words can never show.
8. **Materials** *(optional)* — upload slides/notebook, paste a Google Drive link, or paste text.
   Improves accuracy; **never stored**.
9. Click **Analyze class**.

## 6. Wait a few minutes — it runs by itself

You land on the report page showing **Analyzing…** It refreshes itself; no need to reload.
Transcript-only takes ~3–4 minutes, with video ~6–8 minutes.

> ⚠️ **If it ever looks stuck** (more than 30 minutes) the page says so and shows a **Retry analysis**
> button. One click re-runs it.

## 7. Read the report

**Check the badges at the top first:**

- **🎬 Video verified · N frames** — proof the recording itself was analysed. If it says **Transcript
  only**, findings come from words alone (hover to see why).
- **✓ Self-checked** — every serious finding got a second, adversarial AI review before you saw it.
- **▶ Watch recording** — opens the Vimeo video so you can verify any flag yourself.

**The report has five parts:**

1. **Overall** — two or three sentences on what likely drove the low rating.
2. **Flags** — each problem with severity, an exact quote and its `[HH:MM:SS]` timestamp. Flags with a
   🎬 chip came from the video.
3. **Self-check** — what the second AI reviewer confirmed, softened or removed, with reasons. Nothing
   disappears silently.
4. **Summary to send the instructor** — one opening line with the rating, then bullets: each names one
   specific error and its **Fix**. **This is the only part the instructor receives.**
5. **Re-class recommendation** — PM-only: should this class be re-taught? The instructor never sees it.

## 8. Edit, approve, send

1. **Edit directly** — both the summary and the detailed feedback are editable text boxes.
2. **Or tell the AI what to change** — type "warmer tone" or "focus on the skipped problems" in the box
   under either text and click **Revise**. Repeat until it's right.
3. **Approve & store** — saves both versions (originals kept for comparison).
4. **Copy** the summary and send it to the instructor however you normally do.
5. Click **Mark as sent** — the class shows "Sent to instructor ✓" so the team knows it's done.

> **Nothing is ever sent automatically.** The AI only drafts. A human — you — approves and sends.

## 9. Questions people ask

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does one analysis cost? | ~$0.50 transcript-only, ~$0.70 with video. Each class shows its exact cost; the dashboard tracks monthly totals. |
| Is my materials file stored? | No. Materials and video frames are used in memory for that one analysis, then discarded. Transcripts auto-delete after 20 days. |
| My course isn't in the dropdown | Choose **➕ Other** in the course list and type the name. |
| The badge says "Transcript only" but I ticked video | Hover the badge — it shows why (e.g. the video wasn't reachable). The analysis still completed from the transcript. |
| An analysis failed or looks stuck | Open it — you'll see the reason and a **Retry analysis** button. |
| Who can see the re-class recommendation? | Only signed-in IK staff in this tool. It is never part of the instructor note. |
| Something's wrong / I have an idea | Message **Bishal Roy** (New Programs), or open an issue on this repo. |
